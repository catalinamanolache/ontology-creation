The Net-Zero Industry Act, adopted in June 2024, aims to accelerate the production of net -zero technologies within the European Union and streamline the regulatory environment for these technologies. Its primary goal is to ensure that by 2030, the EU’ s man ufacturing capacity reaches a minimum of 40% of the annual deployment requirements needed to meet the EU’ s 2030 climate and energy objectives.
