In total , 19 countries added new capacity , with Germany in the lead (3.6 GW), followed by Sweden (2.0 GW), France (1.4 GW), and Finland (1.3 GW) (Figure 4). The EU’s total installed onshore wind capacity is now 200.9 GW, an increase of 7% on 2022.
