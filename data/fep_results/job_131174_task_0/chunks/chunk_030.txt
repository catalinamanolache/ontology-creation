Value chain analysis 3.1 Turnover The turnover, GVA and employment are determined using a method that evaluates the wind industry’s economic activity. Specifically, EurObserv’ER considers the monetary flows from the following activities in the value chain: 1) Investment in new installations, 2) Operation and maintenance of existing plants, including newly added plants, and 3) Production and trade of renewable energy equipment (EurObserv’ER, 2024).
