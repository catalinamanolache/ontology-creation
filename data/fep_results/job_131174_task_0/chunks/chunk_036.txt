Even though the share in global exports has increased, it is still lower than the pre -pandemic levels (87% for 2017-2019) as is the value of intra-EU imports (93% for 2017-2019). In 2021 exports increased by 38%, yet the imports to exports ratio was higher than the previous years ( Figure 49). In 2023, the imports to export ratio fell to the pre -pandemic levels but remained higher than 0.1, suggesting that extra -EU imports have captured a share of the EU wind generating sets market.
