The Orcelle project (EUR 9 million) will develop and demonstrate a solution involving wind as the main form of propulsion. The WHISPER project (EUR 9 million) will develop a modular retrofit solution, including a wingsail system to provide wind -assisted propulsion. By developing the novel modular retrofit solution, W HISPER will also test viability by demonstrating eff iciency savings of more than 20%.
